Research

Research and experimentation across AI and emerging technology, from local language models to game development and hardware.

  1. Ongoing 2025

    Offline LLMs

    Building a permanent offline environment for running and keeping language models without internet access, APIs, subscriptions, or cloud services.

    I downloaded open weight models, set up llama.cpp to run them locally, organized the models and software on dedicated storage, and created offline backups so everything can be kept and moved to other machines.

    I am testing what these models can actually do on limited hardware and where their practical limits are.

  2. Ongoing 2025

    Small Language Models

    Experimenting with building small language models from scratch to understand how they work.

    The work covers datasets, tokenization, model architecture, training, inference, quantization, and running the finished models locally.

  3. Ongoing 2026

    AI Game Worlds

    Exploring how language models can be used inside 2D game worlds.

    The idea is to give characters their own behaviour and decision making so they can react to their surroundings and other characters instead of following only fixed scripts.

  4. Ongoing 2026

    AI & Robotics

    Experimenting with language models and small hardware projects.

    The work explores how local models can interact with sensors, physical hardware, and simple autonomous behaviour.
