Research

Research and experimentation across AI and emerging technology, from tailored tools and LLMs to robotics and hardware prototypes. Part of the work focuses on seeing how far LLMs can be pushed locally with limited compute, while other projects explore different ideas and applications.

  1. Ongoing 2026

    Running LLMs locally

    Downloading models, running them offline, and finding where their limits are on ordinary compute.

    The question is how much can be pulled out of very little. I run different tests and experiments to find where a small model stays useful and where it stops being worth the compute it takes.

    I am also using those models to build AI for my 2D game worlds, and putting them into small robotics projects. That part is early, and still going.

  2. Ongoing 2026

    A whole game from one prompt

    A repository that turns a single prompt into a 2D pixel art world: the world, the story, the characters and the mechanics, in one pass.

    Sunrune is the demo that came out of it. A full, detailed version is what I am building now.

    Most of what I have learned has been in the prompt engineering: how to contain and guide a model rather than instruct it step by step. Too many instructions can make the output worse, and a model given a flow to follow often does better. It can also go badly wrong if it takes the wrong direction early, so the prompts have to be precise, with checks and tests along the way, including ways for the model to test and challenge its own work.
