Games.

Complete browser games. Some began as research, and more are in development.

  • Experiment 03

    Sunrune

    Chapter 1: Return to Sender. A top-down story RPG.

    A hooded stranger walks off with the Sunrune that keeps the Gate lit. Pip, a courier cat with one job, decides that something taken belongs back with its sender. A complete chapter with battles, saving and a boss.

    Why it exists
    Built to find out how much AI can make from one simple prompt, using the Rivenside AI Planner with nobody interfering. It took about 8 hours and 5 USD in tokens. Read the research
    How it’s built
    Vanilla JavaScript modules, one canvas and the Web Audio API. No engine, no framework, no build step. The music is synthesised in the browser, and the test suite includes a bot that plays the whole chapter.
    Plays on
    Desktop, phone and tablet, with keyboard, gamepad or touch
    Length
    8 to 12 minutes
    Credits
    Art and sound effects by Kenney, CC0.

  • Experiment 01

    Rust Country

    A single-level platformer in the spirit of Donkey Kong Country.

    You are SCRAP, a small robot alone on a dead Earth. Six beats, start to finish, ending in a boss fight with THE WARDEN. Jump, roll, stomp and fire a laser.

    Why it exists
    How much working software can AI produce from a single prompt, with no human intervention? It set the ceiling everything after it was measured against. Read the research
    How it’s built
    One HTML file of roughly 6,000 lines, with no dependencies. Every sprite, tile and background is drawn in code, all audio is synthesised, and physics runs at a fixed 60Hz. A headless Chrome harness checks movement by searching every sensible input timing.
    Plays on
    Desktop, phone and tablet, with keyboard or touch
    Credits
    Original throughout. No Nintendo names, characters, assets or music.
