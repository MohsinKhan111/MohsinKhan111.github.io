About Rivenside.

Mohsin Khan

Founder

LinkedIn ↗ GitHub ↗ Email

I’m a developer building and running my products independently. I handle development, infrastructure, deployment, maintenance, and everything in between. My products have reached 10,000+ users. Alongside development, I work on AI research and development through small experiments and tools.

I started this company because I wanted a place where I could build and research the things that genuinely interest me. I like working on projects that let me explore different areas of computer science, learn by doing, and see where an idea can go.

It started with basic web development and small 2D pixel art games. Over the years, I built up a large library of private projects of my own. Along the way, I took on freelance work, helping small and medium businesses with websites, IT infrastructure, office setups, and other technical work. That led to work with larger companies, which gave me experience I could bring back into my own projects.

I eventually decided to bring everything under one roof. Today, I mostly work independently, occasionally collaborating with others when a project calls for it.

My interests have expanded quite a bit. I work on small and medium software projects, 2D pixel art games, and increasingly on AI research, particularly how AI models handle context, assumptions, reasoning, and hallucinations. I also build focused repositories and tools for specific tasks rather than trying to make everything do everything.

More recently, I’ve been experimenting with small robotics projects connected to AI. These are mostly prototypes and experiments for now, but that’s part of what I enjoy about building things. There is always something new to explore.
