The short version

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party embeds. It has no forms and no accounts. Its fonts and images are served from this site itself, so visiting it sends no requests to Google or any other third party.

Two things involve personal data: the technical data every web host processes to deliver a page, and whatever you choose to send if you email Rivenside. Both are described below.

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Mohsin Khan as Rivenside. He is responsible for it and is the controller of the personal data described here, within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Rivenside is based in Islamabad, Pakistan.

Contact: mohsinmkhan676@gmail.com

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. Like any web server, it also receives the technical details needed to deliver the page, such as the page requested and your browser type.

  • Purpose: delivering the website reliably and securely.
  • Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: a legitimate interest in operating a secure, working website.
  • International transfers: GitHub may process this data in the United States.
  • Retention: set by GitHub. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If you email Rivenside

Email sent to Rivenside is received and stored by its email provider, Google (Gmail).

  • What: your email address, your name if you include it, your message and any attachments.
  • Purpose: reading and replying to your message.
  • Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns a possible engagement; otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: a legitimate interest in answering messages sent to Rivenside.
  • Retention: up to two years after the conversation ends, unless the law requires a longer period.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device to track you. That is why there is no cookie banner: nothing here needs your consent.

Links to other sites

This site links to GitHub and LinkedIn. Nothing from those services is embedded here, and no data is shared with them. If you follow a link, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you (Article 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Article 16);
  • have your data erased (Article 17);
  • restrict how it is processed (Article 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format (Article 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21).

To use any of these rights, email mohsinmkhan676@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

Nothing is required

You don’t have to provide any personal data to use this website. Emailing Rivenside is entirely voluntary.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and Rivenside does not knowingly collect personal data from them.
